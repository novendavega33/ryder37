Overview

About Me

Dedicated network professional with a passion for designing resilient topologies, maintaining operational uptime, and resolving critical network incidents.

"I work in network operations and enjoy troubleshooting networks, routing, and network infrastructure."

As a Network Engineer / NOC Engineer, my focus centers on maintaining reliable connectivity, managing routing tables, and diagnosing network incidents systematically. I work hands-on with MikroTik routers, dynamic routing protocols (OSPF, BGP), and traffic segmentation using VLANs.

In operational environments, I configure secure site-to-site connectivity using VPN, L2TP, and EoIP, manage bandwidth and traffic prioritization with QoS, and rely on continuous network monitoring and structured network troubleshooting to resolve issues proactively and keep infrastructure running smoothly.
